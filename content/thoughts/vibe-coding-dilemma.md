**Unrefined thoughts** about the misdirection of modern problem solving approach for workflows with the over hype of vibe coding.

### 1. The inherited idea of solution as an app

In the last one or two decades of modern computing before the AI revolution, we are used to the idea of apps being pieces of solutions in our digital workflows. 

The people that are integrated working with digital environments are provided solutions developed and maintained by developers. Our personal computers and mobile devices became the platforms for us to use these applications which helped us solve problems and improve our workflows.

This is a quite expected and normal outcome of how the technology developed naturally over the years, and that's why we still carry this perspective.

We are in an era of technology that we can't even keep up with the development, especially in the AI area.

The concept of agentic artifical intelligence is one of the topics that is quite rapidly introduced and inevitebly integrated in our lives, and yet that rapid impulsive shock caused a misdirection in the understanding and approach by masses as far as I observe. 

As far as I see over the last few months or longer, we tend to inherit the idea of software as a solution and we tend to use agentic AI tools to create vibe coded softwares as solutions to our daily problems. Actually, an agentic AI tool is usually the solution itself most of the time and yet we miss it.

### 2. The revolution in digital solution

The concept of having a large language model, that can roughly be called a form of pure intelligence, of some kind, with action capabilities is a massive revolution in approach.

The main reason we needed software to solve problems in the first place was the need of us to communicate with computer itself, which is running a platform to run software that is also a software. Over the years we tried to understand and communicate with computers, and now the computer answers us back and tries to speak our language which is the breaking change.

Thus, we actually don't NECESSARILY need to build software as the middlewares in the way of reaching solution. In this era, an agent harness is the new approach to solution as a general purpose software that can be whatever solution you want it to be.

### 3. The cost of maintenance

Surprisingly, vibe coding a software (in this post's sence "software as a solution") introducing the cost of maintenance. Normally, in traditional sense, developers build an app and maintain it so that other people use it. This is their task to do, and you use those tools to do your tasks.

The vibe coding hype moves people to also build their own software, which they try to start learning and applying debugging, testing, maintaining their own tool. This creates an extra hurdle in the end, and also considering the fact that wasting tokens to create a software to solve a problem, and solving the problems of the software that was supposed to be a solution but introducing new problems is quite ironic.

### 4. An agent is all you need

The fact that an agent can build software is an amazing and shockingly impressive aspect of agentic tools, but those are capabilities the of it. Not what it directly is.

In most cases, an agent should be used on workspaces by following the already existing and standards that are being shaped day by day of agentic workspaces. (e.g. https://agentskills.io)

### 5. Outcome

My personal observations for myself and for my surroundings *(at least towards my social surrounding and the society that I'm aware of as far as my digital social media algorithm feeds makes me aware of)* suggests that true potential of LLMs and agents are not well understood yet. 

People use it in a way that creates more friction to themselves and eventually costs more tokens, which can be acceptable to some degree considering how fast the technology moving forward and how it's not as easy as it seems to keep up with it.

**TL;DR:** *Your amazing idea might not need to be vibe coded, perhaps an agent harness itself is the solution; and is all you need.*
